# My Favorite Movie: Avengers: Endgame

**Avengers: Endgame** is a 2019 superhero film produced by Marvel Studios and the direct sequel to *Avengers: Infinity War*. It serves as the epic conclusion to the first 11 years of the Marvel Cinematic Universe (The Infinity Saga).

---

## Why I Love It
* **The Storyline:** Watching the remaining Avengers band together for a "time heist" to undo Thanos's snap is thrilling from start to finish.
* **Emotional Payoff:** It wraps up the character arcs of original heroes like Tony Stark (Iron Man) and Steve Rogers (Captain America) in an unforgettable way.
* **The Final Battle:** The "Avengers, assemble" scene where all the dusted heroes return through portals is one of the greatest moments in cinema history.

## Movie Details
* **Release Year:** 2019
* **Directors:** Anthony and Joe Russo
* **Main Cast:**
  1. Robert Downey Jr. (Tony Stark / Iron Man)
  2. Chris Evans (Steve Rogers / Captain America)
  3. Scarlett Johansson (Natasha Romanoff / Black Widow)
  4. Chris Hemsworth (Thor)
  5. Mark Ruffalo (Bruce Banner / Hulk)
  6. Jeremy Renner (Clint Barton / Hawkeye)
